### Formal Verification

NFOT includes a Lean 4 / Mathlib formal-verification component for checking mathematical definitions, assumptions, and propositions associated with the Write-Back Gap and its distributional properties.

The formal component is intended to:

- Verify algebraic properties of the Write-Back Gap.
- Make mathematical assumptions explicit.
- Formalise selected distributional and statistical definitions.
- Identify inconsistencies in mathematical claims.

**Scope limitation:** Formal verification establishes that propositions follow from their stated formal assumptions. It does not establish biological validity, causal effects, or empirical performance.
