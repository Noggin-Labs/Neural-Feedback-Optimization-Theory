# NFOT v7 prospective protocol

**Status:** Proposed draft. No participants, ethics submission, preregistration, or experiment has occurred.

## Primary question

Does randomized post-selection feedback delay change a prespecified subsequent performance or learning outcome beyond ordinary task and participant effects?

## Hypotheses

Null: assigned delay has no effect on the primary subsequent outcome. Alternative: the prespecified delay contrast changes the outcome in the stated direction, with the direction chosen before confirmatory data collection. The same-trial correctness outcome is secondary/negative-control because delay occurs after the response.

## Design

Use a within-participant randomized design with at least two timing conditions and an optional third condition only if engineering feasibility supports it. The exact delays, randomization unit, balance, and block structure remain unresolved decisions requiring qualified supervision and timing pilot evidence. Record assigned delay, achieved software timing, independently measured display onset where relevant, trial order, session, outcome, technical failures, and exclusion reasons.

## Primary outcome and analysis

Choose one downstream outcome before enrollment: for example, correctness over a fixed subsequent block or a fixed learning score. Analyze assigned condition by intention-to-treat using a participant-clustered or mixed-effects model. Report absolute contrasts, intervals, missingness, timing tolerance, and model diagnostics. Treat achieved delay as a manipulation check and secondary exposure.

## Exclusions and missingness

Predefine participant eligibility, incomplete sessions, invalid event sequence, timing out of tolerance, device failure, withdrawal, and missing primary outcomes. Exclusions cannot depend on outcome values. Report all randomized observations and deviations.

## Falsification

The causal hypothesis is weakened by a null or opposite prespecified contrast, failure of timing delivery, or an effect explained by technical artifacts or order. A positive result supports only the defined downstream effect under the tested conditions; it does not establish a biological mechanism.

## Governance

Human research requires appropriate supervision, ethics review, consent/assent, privacy safeguards, and data minimization. No human-subject work is authorized or claimed here.
