Milestones A–G are sequential where claims depend on them: reproducible measurement, historical evidence, robust association, added predictive value, prospective causal evidence, independent replication, and distinct contribution. NFOT currently reaches a software/provenance milestone, not a scientific-breakthrough milestone. These statuses are not averaged into a probability.
