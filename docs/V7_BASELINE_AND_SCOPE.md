This v7 effort preserves all historical v5 and v6 artifacts and adds a separate development layer. It does not overwrite the 444-row derived table, claim source recovery, conduct human-subject research, publish a manuscript, or create a release.

Historical v5 artifacts are the PDF, legacy extractor, derived table, and reported beta/LOSO/AUC claims. V6 artifacts are the guarded extractor, descriptive reanalysis, tests, provenance, validation documents, theory review, and PR #1. V7 artifacts are the source-data blocker audit, claim ledger, formal model, synthetic planning code, literature positioning, protocol, benchmark framework, falsification matrix, mechanism/application reviews, and manuscript draft.

Available evidence is the v5 PDF, 444-row derived table, 12-row timing-prototype table, source code, documentation, requirements, and tests. Unavailable evidence is raw EDF, complete trusted event streams, converter metadata, source-level inclusion/exclusion logs, original held-out predictions, exact historical model outputs, and independent replication data. Two tracked source CSV placeholders are empty two-byte files.

The derived table supports arithmetic checks of its own stored columns. It does not establish event semantics, physical display timing, target-label validity, or independent reproduction of v5 inferential claims. No confirmatory analysis has been completed; all new empirical-looking outputs are either descriptive artifact checks or explicitly synthetic simulations.
